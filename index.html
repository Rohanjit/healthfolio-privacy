In short

Healthfolio keeps your family's health records on your phone. There is no Healthfolio account, no server, no ads and no analytics. We, the developer, never receive your data.

What the app stores

Everything you enter: names and relations of family members, records and their photos, doctors, notes, medicines and doses, health readings, follow-ups and reminders. You choose what to add. The app does not read your contacts, location, call logs or other apps.

Where it is kept

On this phone, in the app's private storage. The records database is encrypted with a key held in the Android Keystore. Device and cloud backup of app data is turned off. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored on the phone.

Internet access

Healthfolio does not ask for internet access and does not send data anywhere by itself. On copies installed from Google Play, the Play Store app may tell Healthfolio that an update is available; that check is handled by Google Play.

Optional folder sync

If you turn on sync, Healthfolio writes a copy of your library into a folder you pick, for example in your own Google Drive, so other phones you choose can read it. That copy lives in your storage account under that provider's terms, not with us. You can encrypt the folder in Settings → Sync. Turning sync off stops new writes; files already in the folder stay until you delete them.

Sharing and export

Records leave the phone only when you share or export them, and only to the app or place you pick, such as WhatsApp, email or a ZIP file. Once shared, the receiving app's privacy terms apply.

Permissions

  • Notifications and alarms, for dose, test and refill reminders.
  • Biometrics, to unlock the app; fingerprints and face data stay with Android and are never seen by Healthfolio.
  • Start-up, to restore reminders after the phone restarts.

Children

Healthfolio is meant for adults managing their family's records. It is not directed at children, and it does not knowingly collect anything from them.

Not medical advice

Healthfolio is a personal filing cabinet. Reference ranges and reminders are for information only and are not a substitute for advice from a doctor.

Deleting your data

Delete any record, person, medicine or reading inside the app, or uninstall the app to remove everything on the phone. Delete the sync folder from your storage account to remove that copy.
